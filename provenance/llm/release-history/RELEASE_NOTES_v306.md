# v306 — Source-authoritative Leningrad Lakes

Leningrad now renders the four authored `lakes` SVG layer paths using their original polyline vertices, at a 20 engine-pixel stroke in lake blue `#209eb1`. Previously the source lake routes were audit-only while nine provisional lake hexside wavelets drew short disconnected bands. On Leningrad only, these provisional lake wavelets are suppressed and the authored paths take their place. No lake polygons or new cells were added. v305 exact rivers remain unchanged. Other maps retain their previous water modes.
