# v2.87 — Restore the v234 water architecture and source sea continuity

## Findings from the newly supplied v234 archive

The procedural rendering architecture was NOT lost. The five-interval shoreline sampler, deterministic water wavelets, connected water-body logic, variable-width river ribbons and river-junction treatment remain in `core/terrain_tiles.js` in v286. The Odessa shortcoming is configuration/representation: 20 shore hexside annotations by themselves create only discrete narrow colored strips, never a connected sea occupying partially submerged hexes.

The historical v234 release explicitly regarded its Kiev 22 river edges as NOT CLEARED FOR GAMEPLAY, and the current v286 Kiev scenario is no more complete. Do not turn bitmap-registered river pixels into gameplay crossing constraints.

## Changes

* Added opt-in `sourceSeaInteriorMode: 'source-registered-contours'` to Odessa and propagated through `maps/composer.js`.
* Reused existing v286 source-registered published-scan purple sea polygons as the connected, partial-hex sea interior UNDER the existing registered hexside coastline wavelet strips; this is source-derived vector data, not generated illustration.
* Coast strips for that source-specific sea use purple `#7772b2` instead of generic cyan. Lake/rivers and other campaigns retain their own colors.
* Added a map-viewer toggle **Odessa source sea interior** to compare old and new layers without affecting authored data.
* Added tests checking source-water preservation, each of the 20 annotated coast hexsides against the shared geometric grid, five-interval shoreline wavelets, visibility toggling, preservation of river geometry and no source fill leaking into standalone Kiev.

## Known limitations — NOT a finished coast or river correction

* The printed-scan sea polygon boundary is source-registered, but not snapped to every hexside. It is a source-evidenced water fill to repair disconnected interior, not a claim that the final coast follows all legal hexsides. The 20 coastline edges remain provisional.
* The 983 printed-bitmap-derived river ink vertices are visual geometry only; they do not establish a connected, reciprocal gameplay hexside river network. The 22 selected v234 Kiev candidate river edges were incomplete and explicitly NOT CLEARED FOR GAMEPLAY.
* Interactions among the sea interior, river estuaries and coastal fringes require further source-side auditing and clipping refinements. Some printed sea extensions may remain visually rough.
* All historical campaigns other than playable Battle for Moscow remain non-playable source-visual candidates.

## Reproduction

Run `python3 serve.py` and open `/maps/index.html`, select Kiev + Odessa, and toggle **Odessa source sea interior**. To regenerate exports: `node scripts/export-campaign-maps.js exports`.
