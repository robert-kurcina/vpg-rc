# v316 — Shared historical CRT policy

The Oberlabs-compatible Battle for Moscow CRT is selected for all five historical map profiles.

- The same 6 odds columns × 6 die results are exposed by `core/historical_crt.js`.
- Source profile: `games/battle-for-moscow/rules.json` and `rules_profiles/shared-crt.json`.
- Registered historical maps: Battle for Moscow; Arduous; Leningrad; Kiev; Kiev–Odessa expansion.
- Game-specific movement rules, terrain/city/river shifts, special support rules, turn structure, replacement schedules, and victory conditions **are not unified**.
- Only Battle for Moscow currently has a complete historical gameplay package. The other four are map configurations and cannot enforce this profile until historical gameplay integrations are created.
- The existing fictional mechanics labs retain their intentionally distinct CRTs.
