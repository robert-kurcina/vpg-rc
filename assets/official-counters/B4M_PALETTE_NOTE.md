Battle for Moscow counter palette: centrally defined in `core/counter_visuals.js`, exported as `WargameCounterVisuals.PALETTES.battleForMoscow`. Its colors are configured by `legacy/ui.js` via the original SVG's independently authorable first and second color regions; no geometry or original SVG text is rewritten. The palette samples the embedded official Battle for Moscow raster reference sheet. Exact color matching requires a calibrated original image, not its browser screenshot.
