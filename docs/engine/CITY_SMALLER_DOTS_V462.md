# Checkpoint 228 / v462 — Smaller city dots

Reduced the explicit city-dot size by half in diameter while preserving their accepted geometry.

## Change
- Previous dot diameter: 6.0 units (radius 3.0)
- New dot diameter: 3.0 units (radius 1.5)

## Unchanged
- 24 dots per city hex
- Dot positions (vertices, quarter points, side midpoints)
- No thick city perimeter
- Shared gameplay, terrain, and CRT
