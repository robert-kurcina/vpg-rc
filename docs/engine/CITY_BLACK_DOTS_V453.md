# Checkpoint 219 / v453 — Black dotted city hex highlights

## User-requested appearance

The former **orange inset dashed** outlines around city hexes are now **black round dots** (as in the supplied visual reference). All five games use the exact same city polygon renderer; no generated images, duplicate per-scenario renderers, or new map sources were introduced.

- SVG stroke: `#000000`, opacity `1`.
- Stroke width/dot diameter: `3` shared viewBox units.
- Dot path: `stroke-dasharray="0.1 7"`, with `stroke-linecap="round"` to turn nearly zero-length dashes into isolated circular marks.
- Polygon geometry: existing inset `rx*.93, ry*.93`; city layer stays at `data-layer-order="10.5"` and is not an outside-the-grid halo.
- Applies to normal map rendering, alternate/preview paths, optional vector artwork, and the deterministically generated common browser bundle.

## Validation

- `node tests/city-black-dots-v453.test.js`: every city polygon across five current SVG exports uses black circles with visible path spacing, correct inset presentation, no city bitmap fallback, and current common-browser source.
- `python3 tests/city-black-dots-raster-v453.py`: samples the **actual exported engine PNG** at Kiev city hex `0706`; confirms several separate black dots and visibly clear gaps, not a continuous line.
- Historical city style tests v445-v447 now check current black dot invariants rather than their superseded old orange requirements.
- All five map SVG and PNG exports regenerated by the existing shared engine at 1800 px width.
- Representative actual-export crop: `rules/audit/visual/kiev-city-black-dots-v453.png`.

**Nonchanges:** city membership/terrain flags, hex geometry and numbered coordinates, rail/river/coast paths, city circles/labels, source tile bitmaps, counters, gameplay mechanics, setup, AI, and shared B4M CRT. The orange Romanian §10.8 source-audit overlay is a separate evidence artifact and has not been repurposed as a city border.

**Priorities:** Arduous German captured-city browser rebuilding remains the active gameplay target. The optional B4M two-counter stacking study remains second-to-last; unified grand-campaign study remains absolute last.
