# Checkpoint 326 / v561 — Naval destination admission before damage

Source review: Odessa §15.1 expressly assigns Fleet movement to Soviet Special Movement. §15.3 allows one ground unit stacked with the Fleet to transfer to another friendly-controlled port or evacuate off-map, with a §15.1 damage check and later regular reinforcement re-entry. It does not expressly assign ground transport to a phase. The Kiev sequence of play does not explicitly resolve this omission. Soviet Special Movement is a reasonable proposed convention, not a confirmed historical rule. No live phase permission is granted here.

The shared naval logistics transaction previously checked enemy occupation and source stacking only if a transported ground unit survived. A reduced unit rolling 1 could therefore bypass those checks in direct module calls. The journal already performs a non-damaging preflight, so this is a component-level defect, not evidence of an exploitable live historical campaign. Now the original unit must be admitted at a legal destination before damage is resolved, regardless of the roll. The trusted stacking callback receives isolated copies; it cannot mutate input state or alter the subsequently resolved unit. The regular admission predicate uses nationality and unit size, which do not change on reduction. Off-map evacuation remains unaffected.
