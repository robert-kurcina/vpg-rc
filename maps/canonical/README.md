# Canonical-coordinate preview (non-production)

`leningrad-preview-v347.json` is generated by `node tools/build-leningrad-canonical-preview-v347.js`.

The preview is **not** a drop-in replacement for `maps/leningrad.json`. It assigns four-digit source-facing identifiers to 186 numbered cells, segregates 11 unnumbered sea geometry cells in `rules/audit/leningrad-canonical-preview-v347.json`, remaps 80 rail connections, 10 rail exits, 23 entry sides, 54 authored-shore sides and 35 numbered sea IDs, keeps 2 water-only shore sides and 11 sea IDs in a separate visual ledger, and preserves 502 physical adjacencies among the numbered cells through universal explicit topology (`geometry.neighborMode: "explicit"`). Its numbering comes from the currently configured map-number translation, not independent proof of every source label.

Do not switch `maps/registry.json` to this map until water-only visual geometry, original source-feature keys, deployment/roster references, rail and shore records, source entry edges, save-file compatibility and scenario action graph policies are migrated together. The user-approved v345 deployment render must remain pixel-identical to the reference crop.
